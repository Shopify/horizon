# Release Notes - Version 4.1.5

This release ships multiple bug fixes and performance improvements.

## What's Changed

### Fixes and improvements

* Fixed blocks with rounded corners being forced to equal heights.
* Fixed the page jumping back to the top after closing the cart drawer on a phone, so shoppers keep their place while browsing.
* Fixed popular countries being listed twice in the country selector.
* Fixed desktop header dropdown menus opening blank while the page is still loading.
* Fixed a thin line of the page background appearing along the bottom edge of the header's top row.
* Fixed app blocks not responding to clicks on the storefront when placed inside a Hero section.
* Improved display on iPhone: pages now extend edge-to-edge, removing the white bars beside the notch in landscape, while content and controls stay clear of the notch and home indicator.
* Fixed group blocks in product cards stacking on mobile when their "Vertical on mobile" setting was turned off.
* Fixed cart quantity inputs losing what the buyer typed when the cart refreshed mid-edit.
* Fixed the cart item quantity input shrinking, most visibly in the cart drawer on tablets.
* Fixed grid layouts, such as the collection list, overflowing the page and overlapping other sections on the storefront.
* Fixed a horizontal scrollbar flashing on slideshows when moving the pointer between the pagination dots and the slide.
* Fixed a slide's video thumbnail showing behind the text when a slideshow places content below the media.
* Multiple improvements for rendering performance.
